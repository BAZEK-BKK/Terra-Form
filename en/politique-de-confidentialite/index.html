Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Terra & Form ("we", "the studio") is committed to protecting the personal data of this website's visitors and of anyone who contacts us. As the studio is based and operates in Thailand, the processing of your personal data is governed by Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA, B.E. 2562 / 2019).

Data controller

The data controller for information collected on this site is:
Terra & Form
Email: contact@terra-and-form.com

Data we collect

When you use the contact form on this site, we collect the information you voluntarily provide:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • The content of your message / project description

Cookies and audience measurement

We use Google Analytics to measure this site's traffic (pages viewed, visitor origin, time spent). These cookies are optional: they are only set after you have given your consent via the banner shown on your first visit. You can change your choice at any time by clicking "Manage cookies" in the footer. Data collected by Google Analytics is anonymized (IP address truncated) and is never used by the studio for advertising purposes.

Purpose of processing

This data is collected solely to respond to your enquiry or project request. It is not used for any other purpose, including marketing, without your explicit consent.

Legal basis

Processing is based on your explicit consent, given when you submit the contact form.

Recipients of your data

Your data is accessible to the Terra & Form team. The contact form uses the third-party service Web3Forms to deliver messages by email; this provider may process data outside Thailand. In accordance with Section 28 of the PDPA, we take steps to ensure this transfer is subject to appropriate safeguards.

Retention period

Your data is kept for as long as necessary to process your enquiry, then deleted within a reasonable period, unless a longer retention is legally required or you request otherwise.

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated at any time to reflect changes to the site or to applicable regulations. The last update date appears at the top of this page.
